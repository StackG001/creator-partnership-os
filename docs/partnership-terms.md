Creator Partnership:
- Creator pays nothing upfront. Creator earns 50% of EVERY sale of their product, however the buyer found it, through Whop revenue share (Dashboard → Affiliates → Revenue share), set per product. There is no affiliate link. Whop pays both sides automatically.
- Fee basis: AFTER_FEES, confirmed 2026-11-11. Whop calculates revenue share after all Whop fees (docs.whop.com/affiliates/setup-rev-share), so the creator gets 50% of what is left after Whop's fees. A one-off GROSS deal stays possible per deal.
- Refunded sales reverse the commission on that sale. iClipmedia sets the price and agrees changes with the creator first.
- iClipmedia owns the product, the Whop listing and the customer list. The creator approves the idea and the final product (within 7 days of each request) before anything goes live with their name.
- Creator promotes during the agreed launch window: a set number of posts on named platforms plus stories/mentions, always disclosing the paid partnership.
- No medical or health claims, no guaranteed-income claims; every factual claim checked and sourced before launch.
- Agreement runs 12 months from launch, renews yearly, either side can end it with 30 days' written notice; commission on sales already made is still paid. If the creator skips the agreed launch posts, iClipmedia may end it and keep selling without their name.
- Nothing is built until both sides sign.
Fixed-fee services (for reference only, not tracked as deals): 50% deposit to start, balance after approval, final files released 3–5 days after the balance clears.
